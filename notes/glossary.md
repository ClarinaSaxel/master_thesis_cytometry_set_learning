## BCR::ABL1

**BCR::ABL1** is a fusion gene/protein strongly associated with CML.
It results from the Philadelphia chromosome translocation.

See: concepts/cml.md

## CML

**Chronic myeloid leukemia.**

A blood cancer associated with BCR::ABL1.

## ELN

**European LeukemiaNet.**

Relevant here because the clinical response data use ELN response
categories such as Favorable, Warning, and Unfavorable.

## TKI

**Tyrosine kinase inhibitor.**

Drug class used to inhibit tyrosine kinase activity. Examples in the
project include dasatinib and nilotinib.
